# Mega Sale — red-hot promo blast

A hypermarket blast poster. The world is sale red, gold starburst stickers explode over it, prices are gigantic, ribbons run diagonal, and a countdown always ticks. Built for promos, flash sales and live-commerce. Everything is LOUD and slightly rotated — this theme shouts, it never whispers.

## Palette semantics
- Sale red is the stage and stays dominant; big cool-off panels are forbidden.
- Gold is the SCREAM voice: starbursts, ribbons, the call-to-action bar. Text sitting on gold is always deep oxblood, never white.
- Deep red voices the countdown tiles — dark blocks that make white digits pop.
- White carries headlines and the giant price; the price wears an oxblood outline so it reads as a printed die-cut sticker.
- Oxblood is ink-on-gold and the price outline; it never appears as a background. Faded white handles crossed-out old prices and fine print.

## Layout language
- Urgency through collision: gold on red, nothing else — never a third hue, never a gradient, never polite whitespace.
- Everything tilts a few degrees; straight is timid. Ribbons run full-bleed and diagonal, repeating their phrase like a marquee.
- The price is the hero and dwarfs everything; the old price hangs above it, struck through; the discount starburst overlaps the price's airspace.
- Headlines crowd — the crowding IS the urgency. Only ribbons, kickers and fine print get letter-spacing.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The starburst: a spiky gold badge with two characters or a percentage inside, rotated, overlapping something.
- The countdown: dark tiles, white digits, gold separators, a spaced kicker line above. A sale that isn't ticking isn't a sale.

## Motion personality
Everything SLAMS: type scales down hard into place, bursts pop with overshoot and rotation, ribbons shoot in from off-frame. Slow fades are off-system.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: blast stickers slapped over the video — a price starburst, a corner countdown, a diagonal ribbon — sticker-sized, never burying the seller.
- As a full page: the full blast poster detonates edge to edge.

## Taboos
A third hue. Gradients. Glassy panels. Polite whitespace. Slow, gentle motion. Small timid prices. White text on gold.
