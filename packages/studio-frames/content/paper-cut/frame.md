# Paper-cut — layered chinoiserie red

Layered paper-cut (jianzhi). Saturated Chinese-red sheets stacked with chamfered knife-cut corners — a lighter plate over a deeper offset echo — gold corner marks framing an ornamental window, vertical couplet strips hung in symmetry, and one gold seal that lands last. Built for guochao, festivals and traditional culture. Ceremonial, centred, engraved in serif; never casual, never flat-web, never gradient-washed.

## Palette semantics
- The base red sheet grounds everything; the lifted lighter-red plate and the deeper echo peeking out behind it ARE the paper-cut layering — every composition sits on this stack.
- Warm rice-paper cream is the main ink on red; a whispered cream voices meta lines.
- GOLD is treated like leaf, rationed: the seal, four corner marks, open cloud arcs, thin rules, and at most ONE gilded word or numeral per composition. Never a gold background, never gold body text.
- There is no third colour — cream is the only second ink; restraint is the style.
- The gold halo belongs to the seal ring alone.

## Layout language
- Centred, symmetric, ceremonial: a spaced gold kicker, an engraved cream headline, cloud arcs grounding the base — held like an invitation, not a feed post.
- Plates are knife-cut: chamfered corners, never rounded; only the seal is round.
- Couplet strips hang vertically in mirrored pairs, written top-to-bottom with generous letterfall — verdicts and blessings live there.
- Red paper carries few words well: one claim per plate, held a beat longer, no dense paragraphs.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The chamfered plate stack — the offset double layer of reds that says "cut paper", on every composition.
- The seal: one round gold chop, slightly rotated, stamped in LAST with its halo — the ceremony's closing act. Gold spent twice is gold wasted.

## Motion personality
Ceremonial order: plates settle first, text rises with dignity, couplets drop like hung scrolls, and the seal stamps down at the end with a firm press. Nothing bounces; nothing hurries.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: cut ornaments hung over the shot — a title banner, a corner medallion, a vertical couplet strip — each carrying its own small stacked red sheets with rationed gold.
- As a full page: the full red sheet unfolds edge to edge under the whole layered ceremony.

## Taboos
Gold backgrounds or gold body text. A third colour. Rounded plates. Casual asymmetric layouts. Two seals or two gilded elements. Bounce. Dense paragraphs on red.
