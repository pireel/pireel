# Kawaii Bubble — candy sticker sheet that bounces

A sticker sheet. Candy-pink paper, puffy white bubbles with THICK plum outlines, speech balloons with little tails, four-point sparkle stars and blush dots scattered like confetti. Built for cute-core and daily-life vlogs. Nothing sits perfectly straight and every entrance bounces — but it is soft without being mushy: the thick ink outline is what keeps the cuteness crisp.

## Palette semantics
- Candy pink is the sheet backing, always visible around the bubbles — a composition that covers it has peeled off too many stickers.
- White is the bubble body, and a white plate ALWAYS carries the thick plum outline plus a soft pink shadow; an outline-less white plate is off-system.
- Warm plum is both text and outline ink — the outline is structural, not decorative.
- Bubblegum pink fills tags, step beads, blush dots and at most ONE bubble per composition; whatever sits on it speaks paper-white. Only that filled element may glow.
- Lavender belongs to the sparkles alone — never to text.

## Layout language
- Every plate tilts a few degrees, alternating direction between neighbours, like a sheet arranged by hand.
- One bubble = one message. Quotes live in speech balloons with tails; steps are round bubble beads strung on a dotted trail; numbers here are step beads, not data — a stat that must appear gets spoken in words inside a bubble.
- Blush dots sit in a pair beside the biggest text, like cheeks; sparkles scatter near corners, a handful at most.
- Headlines live inside bubbles, never naked on the paper; type stays chubby and round — no mono, no tracked capitals.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The thick plum outline on every white plate — the crispness of the cute.
- The speech-balloon tail: the frame talks to you; a quote without its tail is just a card.

## Motion personality
Everything bounces in with an overshoot — balloons hop up, beads pop from nothing in stagger, sparkles arrive LAST. Nothing slides linearly, nothing fades flat, nothing loops.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: peeled stickers — every bubble and balloon carries its own white fill and thick outline so it reads on any shot; pink survives only in small chrome (dots, tags, satellites).
- As a full page: the full candy sheet, bubbles and confetti on pink edge to edge.

## Taboos
White plates without outlines. Two filled accent bubbles. Lavender text. Straight, untilted layouts. Linear slides or flat fades. Data tables. Gradient washes. More than a handful of sparkles.
