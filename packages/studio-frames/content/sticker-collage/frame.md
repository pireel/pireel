# Sticker Collage — die-cut sticker sheet over a clean campus-store page

A sticker sheet slapped over a clean, cool-gray store page. Everything on the page is one of three things: a DIE-CUT STICKER (white-filled, or colour-filled with a thick white rim, soft shadow, slightly tilted), a HIGHLIGHTER MARK swiped across the key words, or a SMALL BLACK LABEL (capsule tags, index dots). The page itself stays light and calm — the stickers carry all the energy. And THE SPEAKER IS A STICKER TOO: this frame mats the person with the same thick white die-cut rim, so person and graphics read as one sheet. Built for campus, unboxing and deal content.

## Palette semantics
- The cool light gray page dominates every screen and stays empty — no grids, no texture; whitespace is part of the look.
- White is both sticker fill and die-cut rim; a coloured or black sticker earns its rim, a white sticker separates by shadow alone. Nothing glows — this is paper.
- Highlighter CYAN is a MARKER, not a fill: it swipes behind key words. The signature sentence pairs a cyan-marked key phrase with a calm gray rest — one pair per screen.
- Neon PINK belongs to the halftone starburst and the one loud badge — ONE pink moment per screen.
- BLACK is a seasoning, never a background: mini label capsules, index dots, and at most one black die-cut board per piece (the quote card), floating with a white rim — never a full-bleed plate.

## Layout language
- Collage placement: small tilts with alternating signs between neighbours, slight overlaps, generous open paper — at least a third of the page stays empty.
- Data lives inside white stickers; black stickers carry labels and verdicts, never data.
- Steps and lists are sticker strips with black index dots, joined by dashed hand-flick doodle arrows.
- The layout respects the person-sticker: content tilts away from the speaker, and a white-rimmed sticker never sits flush against another white rim or against the person — rim-on-rim mushes.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The thick white die-cut rim — on graphics AND on the matted speaker, the same white, the same softness of shadow; graphics never imitate the person's rim, they match it.
- The highlighter pair: marked key phrase + calm gray rest, once per screen.

## Motion personality
Slap and sweep: stickers slap on with a small overshoot and a settling twist, the marker sweeps across its words from the left, the starburst pops LAST and holds. Quick, physical, finite — nothing loops, nothing glows.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: peeled stickers over the shot — a title sticker, capsule tags, marked keywords, one small black label — each carrying its own white fill or rim so it reads slapped onto the video.
- As a full page: the full store page laid down, with the speaker re-entering as a matted sticker standing on it.

## Taboos
Black as a background. Two pink moments. Cyan as a fill. Rim-on-rim stacking. Texture on the empty paper. Glow. Untilted grid layouts. A flat web button for the CTA.
