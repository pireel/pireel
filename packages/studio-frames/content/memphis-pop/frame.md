# Memphis — geometric confetti, hard candy shadows

Memphis-group pop. Cream paper is the playground, and geometric confetti — outlined circles, solid triangles, half-circles, polka-dot patches, squiggle lines — scatters across it. Built for unboxings, creative work and entertainment: playful, but every stroke is a thick, confident ink line. It is never soft, never gradient, never precious.

## Palette semantics
- Deep indigo is the INK: all text, and the outline of every hollow shape and dot patch.
- Pink is the one emphasis: it fills exactly ONE solid shape or one pill-chip per composition. A second pink fill tips the piece into noise.
- Yellow lives only in the hard shadows and at most one squiggle line — never as text.
- Mint fills the quieter shapes, and never carries text either.
- The cream paper stays exposed as the playground; confetti sits directly on it.

## Layout language
- Compositions are deliberately asymmetric: the message sits off to one side, confetti clusters on the opposite side as counterweight. A centred card in the middle is off-system.
- Three to five confetti pieces per composition, mixing hollow, solid and patch — clustered, never sprinkled evenly.
- Type is big and black-weight; headlines align left inside their own plate, never centred in the frame.
- A number may sit RAW on the paper with a shape stacked behind it — no plate needed.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- A white plate always carries BOTH a thick ink outline AND a hard offset shadow — and the shadow is yellow, offset, with zero blur. One without the other breaks the identity.
- Shapes stay pure geometry: perfect circles, sharp triangles, true half-circles, slightly rotated so nothing sits flat.

## Motion personality
Entrances are bouncy and confident — pieces land with a small overshoot, the squiggle draws itself in last as the closing flourish. Nothing drifts aimlessly; once landed, everything holds.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: the composition is a cluster of confetti pieces — a plate, a chip, a couple of shapes — leaving the person clearly visible between them.
- As a full page: the whole sheet of cream paper unrolls edge to edge and the piece becomes a Memphis poster.

## Taboos
Gradients. Soft or blurred shadows. Rounded-blob shapes. Centred symmetric layouts. More than one pink fill. Yellow text.
