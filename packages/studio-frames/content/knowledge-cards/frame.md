# Blueprint — engineering-drawing dark

A technical drawing. Deep navy drafting paper under a faint coordinate grid, shapes STROKED rather than filled, dimension lines with end ticks, leader annotations, and a drawing-title block signing the sheet. Built for knowledge and opinion narration: arguments rendered like schematics — measured, annotated, precise. Never decorative, never soft.

## Palette semantics
- Deep navy is the sheet; the faint grid is its tooth. Near-white is the drafting ink; faded ink voices annotations and labels.
- Electric BLUE is reserved for THE measured thing — one per composition: the hollow-stroked hero numeral, the winning bar's hatched fill, the active tab. A lighter blue seconds it in projections and secondary strokes.
- Emphasis is the 45° hatch fill — engineering's highlight — never a solid colour flood.
- Everything else is line work: hairlines, dashed guides, stroke-only boxes.

## Layout language
- Outline over fill: key shapes are drawn as strokes; giant numerals read as hollow outlined figures, not solid type.
- Everything is annotated: dimension lines with ticks and a measurement in the middle, leader lines from a dot to a note, figure tags and revision marks in wide-tracked mono capitals.
- The title block signs the corner of every sheet — a small bordered strip of drawing metadata.
- Headlines are heavy and tight; every number on screen speaks mono. The voice is a lab notebook, not a poster.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The dimension line: something on every sheet is measured — a claim without a measurement is not a drawing.
- The hollow-stroke hero: the big figure rendered as outline, with the one electric-blue emphasis spent on it or on the hatch.

## Motion personality
Rise and settle, drafting-table calm: the sheet frame fades in, strokes and guides draw themselves, the figure rises and locks. Counts roll up like live readouts. Nothing bounces; nothing decorates.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: detail callouts pinned over the shot — a title block, a corner dimension card, a side spec panel — each drafted on its own small patch of navy grid so strokes stay anchored.
- As a full page: the full sheet of drafting paper, grid and frame edge to edge, the argument drawn as one schematic.

## Taboos
Solid colour floods. Filled shapes where a stroke would do. Unannotated claims. A second electric-blue emphasis. Bounce. Decorative flourishes. Prose-y lowercase labels where drafting capitals belong.
