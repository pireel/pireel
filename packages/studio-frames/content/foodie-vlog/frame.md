# Cream — sticker & candy warmth

Stickers on a cream table. Big rounded white stickers laid slightly tilted, pill-shaped chips, scattered candy-dot ornaments, an occasional blobby plate under a hero number. Warm, tactile, appetizing — built for cooking and food-hunt narration, where the dish on camera is the hero and the graphics are cute props around it, never a wall over it.

## Palette semantics
- Warm cream is the tabletop; white is the sticker stock; blush fills the quieter second sticker and the candy dots.
- Appetite ORANGE is the one crave-point per composition — a badge, a highlighted chip, a hero number. Honey yellow is its softer echo, never louder than the orange.
- Warm cocoa ink writes everything; separation comes from soft shadow and tilt — there are no hairline borders in this kitchen.
- Only the orange element may glow, softly, like warm light on the pass.

## Layout language
- Stickers never sit straight: siblings alternate small tilts, the middle one stays level and overlaps on top — laid by hand, not by grid.
- Lists are flow-wrapped pill chips, not rows; one or two chips take the accent as the highlight.
- Numbers that matter (temperature, minutes, price) sit big on an organic blob plate, framed by a small caption.
- Type is round and friendly, headline-heavy; Chinese body text never gets letterspaced.
- Graphics keep to the lower and side thirds — the food owns the frame.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The tilted white sticker with a soft shadow and no border — the frame's atom; a bordered or dead-straight card is off-system.
- One orange crave-point per composition, exactly.

## Motion personality
Everything pops in with a soft bouncy settle, quick and appetizing — stickers land first, badges and candy dots pop after in stagger. Nothing slides in coldly, nothing glows except the one orange element, nothing overstays.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: props on the shot — a big claim sticker, chip lists, a blob-plate stat — each carrying its own white or blush fill, never painting a cream wall behind them; the dish must show through.
- As a full page: the cream table itself, sticker cluster and candy dots edge to edge — brought out only for a deliberate scene card.

## Taboos
Covering the food. Hairline borders. Straight, un-tilted sticker grids. Two orange crave-points. Letterspaced Chinese. Cold linear slides. A full-bleed background behind overlay props.
