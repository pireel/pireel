# Y2K — chrome-blue bubbles and orbit rings

Early-2000s cyber pop. Frosty silver-blue paper, glossy white bubbles each wearing a single highlight dot, an electric-blue ORBIT ELLIPSE physically wrapping the headline, and pink sparkles scattered like lens glints. The whole frame feels like a skinned music-player UI. Built for fashion, streetwear and music content. Bubbly and airbrushed-clean, but built flat: outlines, one glow, round geometry — no gradients, no chrome textures.

## Palette semantics
- Icy blue is the atmosphere and stays visible around every composition — a full-bleed panel kills the airiness.
- White forms bubbles, capsules and chips, and every bubble gets exactly ONE white highlight dot near its upper shoulder — the gloss.
- Electric BLUE is the voice: it draws the orbit ring, fills the primary capsule, and owns the glow — which attaches to the ring and the primary capsule ONLY.
- Hot PINK is strictly decorative: sparkles and one secondary ring, never text, never a fill.
- The deeper blue bubble tone drifts in the background and never carries text.

## Layout language
- Headlines are wide, italic and optimistic — stretched horizontally as the signature; the stretch does the work, so the italic never gets letterspaced.
- The orbit ellipse wraps the focal thing — headline, number, CTA — sized loose so the words stay readable inside it; the pink secondary ring layers behind at the opposite rotation.
- One glossy focal object per composition: a headline in orbit, a quote in a bubble, a CTA in a capsule — never two.
- Capsules run full-round; nothing in this system has a sharp corner, ever. The CTA is the double-outline capsule — blue fill, white gap, outer blue line, all three layers or it is not the Y2K button.
- Window idioms (messenger panes, notepad windows) may appear as rounded white panes with title bars — the skinned-UI memory.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The orbit ellipse around the focal object, glowing, with its pink counter-ring behind.
- The highlight dot on every bubble — gloss is the period aesthetic; a matte bubble is off-system.

## Motion personality
Everything inflates: focal objects pop in with a bouncy overshoot, rings settle DOWN like a lens focusing, sparkles pop in stagger and at most two blink finitely, ending visible. The composition freezes glossy — nothing slides linearly, nothing loops.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: skinned widgets floating over the shot — a title bubble with its ring, a corner counter capsule, a player chip — each with its own glossy fill, outline and highlight dot.
- As a full page: the full skinned screen launches — icy blue edge to edge, orbit ring and bubbles like a player theme.

## Taboos
Gradients or chrome textures. Sharp corners. Pink text or pink fills. Glow beyond the ring and primary capsule. Letterspaced italics. Two focal objects. Matte bubbles without the gloss dot. Infinite blinking.
