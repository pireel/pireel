# Particle — deep-space dust that gathers into meaning

A deep field. Near-black indigo space, a scattered dust of tiny glowing particles, a few of them joined by hairline constellation lines, and a soft nebula halo breathing behind the hero text. There are NO panels, NO cards, NO borders — text floats directly in space, and structure is implied by where the dust gathers. Built for science, cosmos and immersive narration. Every composition tells the same story: chaos converges into one luminous statement.

## Palette semantics
- The void is the ground and must stay dark and mostly empty — emptiness IS the composition; large fills lighten it into nothing.
- Starlight ivory-blue carries the reading text; faded starlight voices captions.
- Nebula BLUE owns the glow: the halo, the brightest particles, hero numerals, constellation strokes. The hero element wears a soft light; captions never do.
- Warm star GOLD is the rare second voice — a few warm particles and the single emphasized word in a quote. Never body text, never a fill.
- Lines exist only as drawn constellation strokes — never as box borders.

## Layout language
- One luminous statement per composition, floating off-centre in the void; the halo sits behind it like weather.
- Around the statement, a loose dust of small dots at varied sizes and brightness — clustered where meaning is, thin elsewhere.
- Charts and timelines are constellations: a drawn line with node stars, the payoff node the brightest; no axes, no grid — the void is the plot area.
- Small wide-tracked latin capitals serve as kickers; giant numerals speak mono and glow blue.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- Convergence: particles fly IN from their own directions and settle into place — the composition assembles like dust becoming a constellation.
- The frameless float: any box, card or border around content collapses the space back into a slide.

## Motion personality
Gravitational calm: the halo breathes in, dust converges softly with stagger, lines draw themselves, the brightest point lights LAST. Nothing bounces; nothing slides in as a block; if a composition feels empty, it is correct.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: pockets of void drifting over the shot — a luminous line, a corner constellation, a short caption — each gathering on its own soft-edged dark wash (a dim halo, never a bordered card).
- As a full page: the whole deep field, edge to edge, one statement burning in it.

## Taboos
Panels, cards, borders. Bright or busy backgrounds. Gold body text or gold fills. Glow on captions. Bouncing or block slides. Axes and grids on charts. Crowding the void.
