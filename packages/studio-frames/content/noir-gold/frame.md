# Noir — maison editorial on near-black

A fashion-house invitation. Warm near-black, champagne gold, serif display, extreme whitespace. Everything is centred, small, and expensive. Built for brand, light-luxury and drop teasers. The house whispers — hierarchy comes from spacing and tracking, never from adding colour or winning a size war.

## Palette semantics
- The ground is warm near-black, never pure black; ivory is the ink.
- Champagne GOLD is precious: hairline rules and frames, one gilded numeral or word per composition, thin outlines. NEVER a large gold fill. A darker gold seconds the rules.
- The rare gold halo may breathe around the single gilded thing; nothing else lights up.
- Panels barely exist — when needed, a surface sits only one breath above the ground.

## Layout language
- Centred and framed: a double hairline frame (gold outside, ivory inside) encloses the composition like an invitation card.
- Serif headlines run large but LIGHT, with widened tracking; quotes turn italic. Labels are tiny latin capitals with EXTREME letterspacing.
- The ornament rule — hairline, diamond, hairline — punctuates; a small gold diamond marks the current or chosen thing.
- One element per composition, huge margins. Numerals are serif-elegant and thin, gilded when they matter. The CTA is an outlined gold pill with two spaced characters — no fill, no shout.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The double frame around the composition — the invitation's edge.
- The single gilded element and the whisper discipline: if a second thing tries to shine, neither does.

## Motion personality
Arrive, never pop: slow fades, the smallest drifts, hairlines drawing themselves, the diamond arriving last. A numeral may ease its letterspacing as it appears. Nothing bounces, nothing pulses.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: gilded placards floating over the shot — a serif plaque, a corner numeral card, a hairline-framed note — each on its own patch of warm dark so the gold still whispers.
- As a full page: the full invitation — near-black edge to edge, double frame, one centred statement.

## Taboos
Large gold fills. Bold heavy serif. Two shining elements. Crowded layouts. Pure black. Bounce or pop. Loud buttons. Size-war hierarchy.
