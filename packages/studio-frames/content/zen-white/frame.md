# Zen — one line, one dot, and a page of silence

Radical emptiness on warm paper. A single small serif text block placed at a golden-ratio point, ONE hairline as the only structure, ONE cinnabar dot as the only colour — pressed like a seal. The vast majority of the surface stays blank, and the blankness IS the message. Built for tea, craft, philosophy, slow living. If a composition feels too empty, it is exactly right.

## Palette semantics
- Warm off-white is the page itself. There are no panels and no cards.
- Soft ink carries all text; a faded ink voices captions and annotations.
- Cinnabar red appears EXACTLY ONCE per composition, as a small round dot — a seal. It is never a text colour, never a fill, never twice.
- A warm grey may colour one small Latin caption at most.
- The single hairline is drawn in near-invisible ink. Corner radius, shadow and glow are zero by decree.

## Layout language
- Anchor the text block at a golden-ratio point of one axis and near an edge on the other. Nothing is ever centred both vertically AND horizontally.
- One hairline per composition — horizontal or vertical, never both. It is the pause of the page.
- Side annotations run vertically, like spine text on a book, hugging an edge.
- Small captions keep far away from the main block; the distance between them is the composition.
- Type is serif throughout, modest in scale and weight, generously tracked. Numerals stay light — never bold, never huge.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The seal: the cinnabar dot rests ON the hairline or closes a text block — the full stop of the composition.
- The silence: most of the surface must remain empty. Filling it is the one unforgivable move.

## Motion personality
Only slow fades and the smallest drift. The hairline draws itself unhurried; the dot always arrives last. Nothing else moves.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: quiet slips of warm paper carry a line, a rule, a dot — placed clear of the speaker so the silence travels with them.
- As a full page: the whole page of warm paper, and the emptiness owns the screen.

## Taboos
A second colour. A second hairline. Bold numerals. Boxes, cards, shadows, glows, rounded corners. Central symmetry. Anything fast.
