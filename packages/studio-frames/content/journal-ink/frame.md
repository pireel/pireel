# Journal — front page in newsprint

A newspaper front page. Warm newsprint, black serif ink set in columns between hairline rules, a masthead over a double rule, and one red editor's stamp. Built for deep dives, roundups and commentary. Credible, dense, composed — DENSITY IS THE AESTHETIC: whitespace here reads as unset type, so columns get filled, with grey placeholder bars standing in for body copy where real text would drown the point.

## Palette semantics
- Newsprint carries black serif ink; the grey of the second surface doubles as the fake body-text bars that keep the page dense.
- Seal RED is the editor's hand, stamped ONTO set type — a rotated stamp, a circled verdict, a red index numeral — at most twice per composition. Red never sets body text.
- Link BLUE is the rare second ink for the odd reference or byline accent.
- Corners are print-square; shadows barely exist; nothing glows on paper.

## Layout language
- Front-page hierarchy: masthead over a double rule, a centred banner headline, a deck line between hairlines, then columns split by hairlines and filled dense.
- Verdicts live in editorial furniture: evaluation tables in a double-rule sandwich, pull-quotes led by a long dash, letters-to-the-editor Q&A, rolling-credit lists with dotted leaders.
- Meta speaks in small mono — volume numbers, dates, page references — like the paper's plumbing.
- The lead claim is always the banner; everything else files in under it at column scale.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The double rule: the thick-thin (or six-point double) rule that opens the masthead and sandwiches tables and pull-quotes — the page's skeleton.
- The red stamp landing like a chop — scaled down onto the page with a hard press, the editor's voice spent on the one verdict that earns it.

## Motion personality
Paper motion: quick fades and short slides along the column axis, rules drawing themselves, the red stamp pressing in last. No scaling, no bouncing — except the stamp's press.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: clippings over the shot — a headline strip, a boxed figure, a short margin column, the stamp — each set on its own scrap of newsprint with hairline rules.
- As a full page: the full front page goes to press — masthead, banner, columns and stamps edge to edge.

## Taboos
Empty airy layouts (unset type). Red body text or red beyond two marks. Glow. Rounded corners. Bounce. Sans-serif headlines. A shouting CTA — the paper closes with a modest classified notice.
