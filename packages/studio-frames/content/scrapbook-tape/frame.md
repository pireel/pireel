# Scrapbook — kraft collage, tape-pinned memories

A handmade scrapbook page: warm kraft paper, white polaroid cards, translucent washi tape and sticky notes, all pinned down slightly crooked. Built for diary vlogs, travel logs and hauls. Nothing aligns to a grid on purpose — every element is rotated a degree or three and overlaps a neighbour, like objects physically glued onto a page. The charm is imperfection.

## Palette semantics
- Kraft is the page: leave large areas of it exposed; never cover it edge to edge with one panel.
- Polaroid white voices the cards; sticky-note yellow is reserved for notes and checklists, never a page background, never a text colour.
- Warm orange is the writer's hand: crooked underlines, checkmarks, one highlighted word, an oversized quote mark — at most two touches per composition.
- Green is TAPE ONLY — always translucent, always crossing a corner or a note's top edge. Never text, never a fill.
- The muted tone handles captions, dates and struck-through done-items.

## Layout language
- Everything is slightly rotated and physically layered; a flat, unrotated white card breaks the system.
- Paper corners, not pills: anything rounder than a paper corner reads as plastic.
- Headlines live INSIDE cards or notes — text floating raw on kraft looks unglued.
- A polaroid's thick bottom margin IS its caption strip; the caption is written there, small and tracked.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- Every lifted piece of paper carries one real drop shadow AND a rotation — flat or straight means unglued.
- Tape pins things: a translucent green strip across a corner or a top edge is how anything attaches to the page.

## Motion personality
Pieces land like being pressed onto the page — a settle with the tape arriving just after; the hand-drawn underline draws itself. No gloss, no glides.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: the live shot IS the page — polaroids, notes and tape strips glue directly onto the video, each carrying its own paper and shadow. No kraft backdrop behind them.
- As a full page: the kraft sheet comes out and the piece becomes a spread from the journal itself.

## Taboos
Grid-perfect alignment. Gradients. Glow. Pill corners. Green text. A centred hero card. Clean sans-serif sterility.
