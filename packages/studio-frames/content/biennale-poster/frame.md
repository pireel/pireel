# Biennale — constructivist poster, print-hard

A gallery poster pinned to the wall. Flat biennale yellow, ink black, square corners, hard offset shadows — and TYPE IS THE LAYOUT: headlines run oversized and are allowed to crop at the edges like a print that outgrew its sheet. Built for manifestos, hot takes and launches. Loud, flat, typographic; nothing here is ever soft, blurred, or gradient-washed.

## Palette semantics
- The yellow is the brand. It stays exposed — big panels covering it are covering the poster itself.
- Ink black is BOTH text and accent. Emphasis is INVERSION — an ink plate with yellow type — used exactly once per composition. There is no third colour anywhere in the system.
- White plates carry a hard ink offset shadow with zero blur; a white plate without that shadow is a stray sheet, not part of the poster.
- Mono catalogue chrome (issue numbers, dates, spine text) speaks in faded ink.

## Layout language
- One message per composition, set at maximum size; whitespace is part of the shout. Cropping a headline at the frame edge is a feature, not an accident.
- Print chrome frames the piece: a vertical mono spine along one edge, a numbered footer index above a thick ink rule, spaced-caps catalogue lines. Latin labels wear wide tracking.
- Comparisons are hard splits — one half yellow with ink type, the other half ink with yellow type, a small rotated seam badge riding the joint.
- Rules are structural: a thick ink rule closes a composition; hairlines divide; nothing floats unanchored.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The inverted ink plate: every composition spends its single inversion on the one thing that must land — a second line, a verdict, a CTA. Two inversions cancel each other.
- The oversized crop: at least one element per piece runs bigger than polite and touches or crosses an edge.

## Motion personality
Print does not ease. Elements slam in fast and hard from opposing sides and freeze like a poster pasted up; chrome fades in last. Nothing bounces, nothing drifts, nothing loops.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: poster fragments pinned over the shot — a title slab, a numbered corner plate, a caption strip — each on its own flat yellow or ink ground with the hard shadow, cropping boldly but never burying the speaker.
- As a full page: the whole poster — yellow edge to edge, giant cropped type, spine and footer chrome — and the frame IS the print.

## Taboos
Gradients. Blur. Rounded corners. Soft shadows. A third colour. Two inversions on one composition. Bouncy easing. Timid, contained type.
