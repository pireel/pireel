# Botanical — herbarium field notes

A herbarium sheet. Warm rice paper, one fine-line botanical sprig drawn in moss-green ink, a white specimen label pinned by slanted kraft tapes, latin catalogue lines in spaced capitals, and a single terracotta wax-seal dot pressed like a collector's mark. Built for nature, gardening and slow living: hand-catalogued, airy, daylight-quiet. Nothing here glows, bounces, or saturates.

## Palette semantics
- Rice paper is the sheet; white is the specimen label stock, kraft the tapes that pin it.
- Deep leaf-green ink carries the serif text; a faded green voices catalogue lines and margin notes.
- Moss green is almost exclusively a STROKE — the drawn sprig, leaf glyphs, thin rules. It never fills a background.
- Terracotta appears exactly ONCE per composition, as the small round wax seal. Nothing else may be terracotta.
- Light is daylight: soft shadows barely there, no glow anywhere.

## Layout language
- Asymmetric two-column air: the drawn sprig owns one column, serif text owns the other, a single vertical hairline between them. Never crowd both columns with text.
- Type is serif at a light-to-medium weight only, airy line-height, gentle tracking — pressed, never shouted. Catalogue chrome runs in wide-tracked latin capitals with numbers and dates.
- Labels are pinned objects: a card sits under two slanted tapes, slightly imperfect, like a hand mounted it.
- One specimen per composition — name it, date it, note one habit; the field notebook never lists two flowers on one sheet.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The drawn sprig: a fine curved stem with a few simple leaves, rendered as strokes that draw themselves in — the sheet's living thing.
- The wax seal closing the entry: the terracotta dot lands last, like a signature.

## Motion personality
Slow and botanical: the sprig draws in stroke by stroke, text fades up unhurried with generous stagger, the wax seal presses in at the end. Nothing pops, nothing races.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: pressed labels laid over the shot — a specimen card under its tapes, a corner catalogue line, a margin note with the wax dot — keeping well clear of the speaker.
- As a full page: the complete herbarium sheet, sprig and label and catalogue chrome on rice paper edge to edge.

## Taboos
A second terracotta mark. Moss green as a fill. Bold black-weight type. Glow or bounce. Symmetric centred layouts. Crowding the sprig's column. Saturated colour blocks.
