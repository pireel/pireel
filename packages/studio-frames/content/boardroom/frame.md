# Boardroom — consulting deck precision

A top-tier consulting deck. Cool white paper, navy ink, one corporate blue, one alert red, and absolutely zero decoration. Built for business analysis, workplace and finance content. The aesthetic is precision spacing: whitespace is deliberate, alignment is ruthless, nothing rotates, nothing bounces, nothing glows.

## Palette semantics
- Cool boardroom white is the paper; pure-white panels lift off it with a faint shadow; a recessed grey voices de-emphasised bars and disabled states.
- Navy ink carries everything that matters; the muted tone handles footnotes, page numbers, axis labels and owners.
- Corporate blue is DATA INK, not decoration: the top strip, section numbers, chart fills, positive deltas, the one highlighted status.
- Alert red marks negatives, risks and frozen items — at most one red element per composition.
- Hairlines rule everywhere: header underlines, row separators, footnote rules, near-invisible chart gridlines. Nothing luminous, ever.

## Layout language
- Precise left-aligned grid. Headlines are ACTION TITLES — full-sentence conclusions ("Acquisition cost doubled; repeat purchase balanced the books"), never topic words ("Cost analysis").
- Numbers are the loudest thing on the page: figures large and tabular, labels small, tracked and muted above them.
- Chrome text — section numbers, page references, sources — is small, muted and slightly tracked, like a deck's footer.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- Every panel is chrome-framed: a full-width accent strip along the very top, a section header row beneath it, a footnote hairline at the bottom. The strip-header-footnote frame IS the identity.
- Conclusion-first titling: if the headline could caption any slide, it is wrong; it must state THIS slide's finding.

## Motion personality
Entrances are measured and quiet — rules draw in, rows step in one after another, numbers count up with restraint. Nothing overshoots.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: each piece is its own chrome-framed white exhibit — an action-title bar, a KPI corner card, a source footnote — floating clear of the presenter.
- As a full page: the composition becomes a full deck slide, paper edge to edge, the page itself the unit of communication.

## Taboos
Rotation. Bounce. Glow. Decoration without data. Topic-word headlines. More than one red element. Centred prose paragraphs.
