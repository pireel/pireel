# Glass — frosted layers floating over light

Frosted glass over coloured light. A deep slate room is lit by two huge blurred colour orbs — ice blue and violet — and translucent glass panes float ON TOP at staggered depths, catching that light. The light always comes first; the glass exists to catch it, the content to sit on the glass. Built for product reveals, AI and gadget reviews: premium, cool, luminous, never flat.

## Palette semantics
- The dark slate room shows only around the light's falloff — it is atmosphere, not a surface.
- The two orbs are the only large colour fields allowed: one ice blue, one violet, always blurred soft, always bleeding off the edge, always on opposite corners.
- Glass is white at a whisper of opacity, blurred behind, edged by a thin bright line — and its TOP edge is brighter than the rest, reading as a lit rim.
- Ice blue also owns the data: the hero numeral, the leading bar, chip dots — solid and glowing. Whatever sits on solid blue speaks in the room's dark tone.
- Faded ivory voices labels; violet never carries text.

## Layout language
- Panes stack like sheets on a lightbox: one MAIN pane anchored off-centre, one SMALL pane overlapping a corner of it at higher depth. A single centred card is off-system; symmetry is off-system.
- Every pane wears the same generous rounding — no exceptions, no mixed corner language.
- Data reads as instruments: mono numerals, one glowing leader among quiet frosted siblings, small chip labels with a lit dot.
- Depth is the hierarchy: what matters floats higher and overlaps; nothing important sits flush.

## Signature (miss one and it is off-system)
- The z-story — light first, glass second, content third. Every composition repeats this order; glass with no light behind it is just a grey box.
- The lit top rim on every pane, and at most ONE solid glowing element per composition.

## Motion personality
Rise and settle, like panes being laid on a lightbox: the orbs breathe in first, panes float up and land softly in depth order, the glowing element lights last. Nothing snaps, nothing bounces.

## Two faces
- Overlaid on footage: floating panes over the shot — a title pane, a corner stat chip, a side card — each carrying its own frosted body and lit rim so the glass reads against the video, leaving the speaker in view.
- As a full page: the whole lit room — slate, both orbs, a layered stack of panes — and the frame becomes the product stage.

## Taboos
Glass without light behind it. A centred single card. Symmetric grids. More than one glowing element. Sharp corners. Violet text. Hard shadows or hard entrances.
